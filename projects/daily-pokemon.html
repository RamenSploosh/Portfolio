About This Series

Welcome to my Daily Pokemon project! I started this back around July 28th, 2025 and kept it going every day, for nearly a year, I took a break from it on June 19th, 2026. Its a challenge I gave myself to draw 1 Pokemon a day, each in a unique art style/medium. It was a fun way to challenge myself, and I plan on continuing it one day, untill I've drawn 'em all!
