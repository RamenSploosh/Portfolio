Pikmin New Fortune

A collection of work from a collaborative online project I was part of, called Pikmin: New Fortune. It was a fanhack of the Nintendo game Pikmin 2, and was intended to act as a fanmade sequal to the game with tonnes of new charactors, enemies, and locations added to it! Sadly, the project dissolved due to drama and in-fighting. It was a real shame, lotta talented folks were hard at work on it.

Gallery

Here's a collection of concept art i created for the project. It includes enemy designs and the design for the new spaceship hat was going to be included, created in a junkyard from bits of other ships.

I was also asked to help them design a logo for the project, which I took very seriously. I re-created the logo from the origional series from scratch, and also recreated the look of the in-game text for the subtitle. I had a lot of fun working on this, it was areal shame the project fell apart.
