The Sticky Note Project

An ongoing side project of mine, started back around 2022. Whenever I'm working at The Shadowbox Theatre selling tickets, reviewing plays, doing tech for a show, etc, I'll often doodle on stickey notes, and pop them onto the wall behind the box office. After however long its been, the little gallery there has really expanded, and I've done all sorts of fun experiments! Here's to many more years of small, square, doodles!
