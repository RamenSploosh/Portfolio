About This Series

Making zines has been a newer exploration for me, but I've been making as selling them lately at fairs and on comission at the Shadowbox theatre, and hopefully at other locations, soon.

Gallery

As of creating this site, this is my most recenty finished zine. It's the first in a series of variety zines, that I want to fill with weird parody comics, stories from my life, poems, and just whatever else strikes my fancy! I've been selling them at fairs and such for 5$.

This was my first zine, done as a project for a university collage class. I challenged myself to make a zine of various collages using ONLY one image as my source material, and see how weird I could get with it. I hope to continue this series soon, it was incredibly fun to make.

Some behind the scenes looks at the zines in person! This is still a very new journey for me, and I'm excited to get many more of these done soon!
